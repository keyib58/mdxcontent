**The iGaming Industry Is Facing Content Saturation**

For years, the iGaming industry measured value by scale. The more suppliers, integrations, and game titles an operator could offer, the stronger the commercial proposition appeared to be.

Today, that model is changing.

iGaming lobbies are more crowded than ever, while player attention is increasingly limited. With so many similar titles competing for visibility, game discoverability has become one of the biggest challenges in the iGaming industry. In many cases, adding more familiar games does little to change player behaviour or improve business performance.

That is why operators are now asking a different question:

**What content can help us stand out from competitors?**

This shift is moving the industry away from volume-led strategies and toward **portfolio differentiation**.
