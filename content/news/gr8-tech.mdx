**KingMidas Games Partners with GR8_TECH to
Expand Global Distribution of Next-Generation Games**


![image](https://publiccdn.kingdomhall729.com/website/images/0902_news.png)

**Dubai, UAE, 31 August 2026** – KingMidas Games has announced a new partnership with GR8_TECH, a leading B2B iGaming technology provider, to expand the global distribution of its growing games portfolio.


Through the partnership, GR8_TECH will integrate KingMidas Games’ portfolio of cutting-edge titles into its aggregation platform, GREAT_CASINO AGGREGATION, spanning Slots, Classics and other genres. The integration will make KingMidas Games’ content accessible to more operators worldwide, strengthening its global reach while giving GR8_TECH’s partners greater access to innovative and engaging gaming experiences.


**Sargis Khachatryan, Commercial Director at KingMidas Games**, commented: “We have built a great relationship with GR8_TECH, and this partnership is a natural next step for both brands. As we continue to expand our presence globally, working with partners that share our ambition for innovation and quality is incredibly important. Our mission is to empower operators with games that engage players from all over the world, and GR8_TECH’s extensive network gives us another strong platform to deliver on that mission.”


**Lusine Khudaverdyan, Head of Casino Business at GR8_TECH**, said: “The golden touch is a big claim to put in your name, but KingMidas Games backs it up, consistently turning player attention into operator revenue with their titles. We're happy to add their portfolio to GREAT_CASINO AGGREGATION.”


The partnership marks another step in KingMidas Games’ global distribution strategy, bringing its growing portfolio to more operators and players while reinforcing both companies’ commitment to delivering innovative and high-performing content across international markets.
